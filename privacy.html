Privacy policy

Family Wall · effective 21 September 2026 · updated 24 October 2026

What this app is

Family Wall is a personal, self-hosted app used by a single household. It runs on computers in that household's home and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Calendar and email (the household's wall account). With the account owner's consent, the app uses one Google account controlled by the household. It uses Google's Calendar API (calendar.readonly, calendar.events) to read that account's calendars and to create, change and delete events on them when a household member asks it to, and Gmail (gmail.modify, gmail.send) to read the messages household members send to that account, label them as handled, and reply with a receipt.

Home controls (Google Nest). On the household's kitchen display, the app can also show and control the household's own Google Nest devices, such as the thermostat. With the account owner's consent it uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read those devices' status and change their settings, and Google Cloud Pub/Sub to receive their status updates.

How the data is used

Calendar events are used to show them on the household's own displays and on household members' phones through a private, sign-in-protected connection. Messages household members send to the wall account are read to carry out what they ask (for example adding an event or a grocery item). To do that, the text and attachments of those messages are sent to an AI model provider (Meta), and the titles of events on a shared family calendar are sent to an AI classification service (TypeSafe AI) to decide which family member each event is for; these providers process the data only to return the result. Nest device data is used only to show and control those devices on the household's own displays. Events and a record of handled messages are kept in a database on the household's computer so the display keeps working without the internet.

Sharing

Data read from Google is not sold, not used for advertising, and not sent to any server operated by the developer. Apart from the AI processing described above, it is not shared with third parties. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the household's own computers.

Retention and removal

Cached events are replaced on every sync and cover a rolling window of about a year. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the app's data folders removes everything it has stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

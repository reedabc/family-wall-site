Privacy policy

Family Wall · effective 21 September 2026

What this app is

Family Wall is a personal, self-hosted app used by a single household. It runs on a computer in that household's home and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent, the app uses Google's Calendar API with read-only access (calendar.readonly) to read calendars and events visible to one Google account controlled by the household. It cannot create, change or delete anything in Google Calendar.

How the data is used

Calendar events are used for one purpose: showing them on the household's own display and on the household's own devices on its home network. Events are cached in a database on the household's computer so the display keeps working when the internet is unavailable.

Sharing

Data read from Google is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not sent to any server operated by the developer or anyone else. It never leaves the household's own computer and home network.

Retention and removal

Cached events are replaced on every sync and cover a rolling window of a few months. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the app's data folder removes everything it has stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

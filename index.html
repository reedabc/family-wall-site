Family Wall

A private family calendar display.

Family Wall is a personal, self-hosted app that runs on a single computer inside one household. It shows that household's shared Google calendars, a meal plan and a grocery list on a screen at home.

It is not a public service: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to download here. The app reads calendar events (read-only) from one Google account that the household controls, and displays them on that household's own screen.
